Open-source reference architecture

Agentic identity, assembled

The plumbing that lets an AI agent act on your behalf — without ever holding your passwords or your provider tokens. User authentication, delegated authorization, third-party token brokering, human-in-the-loop approval, and fine-grained RAG authorization, built from Keycloak, OpenFGA, and OpenBao and reachable over MCP. An OSS answer to "Auth0 for AI Agents."
